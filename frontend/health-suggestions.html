Heart Health Tips

Simple, everyday habits that support a healthy heart — because prevention matters as much as detection.

Eat Heart-Smart

Small dietary shifts add up to big protection over time.

  • Reduce salt, fried food & saturated fat
  • Add more fruits, vegetables & whole grains
  • Choose healthy fats like nuts & olive oil

Stay Active Daily

You don't need a gym — consistency matters more than intensity.

  • 30 minutes of brisk walking, most days
  • Take stairs instead of elevators when possible
  • Stretch and move every hour if you sit for work

Avoid Tobacco & Limit Alcohol

Smoking is one of the single largest controllable risk factors for heart disease.

  • Quitting smoking rapidly lowers heart risk
  • Limit alcohol to moderate levels
  • Avoid secondhand smoke exposure where possible

Monitor Your Numbers

Blood pressure and cholesterol often show no symptoms until it's serious.

  • Check blood pressure regularly
  • Get cholesterol & blood sugar tested yearly
  • Track and manage your weight over time

Sleep & Stress Matter

Chronic stress and poor sleep directly strain your cardiovascular system.

  • Aim for 7–8 hours of sleep nightly
  • Practice deep breathing or meditation
  • Take breaks — don't let stress become chronic

Routine Check-Ups

Catching risk factors early is far easier than treating advanced disease.

  • Annual health check-ups after age 30–40
  • Family history? Screen earlier and more often
  • Don't ignore persistent chest discomfort or fatigue
