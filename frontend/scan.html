How Was This Diagnosed?
1
Image Preprocessing

Your angiogram is resized and normalized to match the format the AI models were trained on.

2
Vessel Segmentation

A U-Net neural network (ResNet34 backbone) traces the coronary vessel tree pixel-by-pixel, learning what a vessel looks like from thousands of labeled training images.

3
Stenosis Segmentation

A second, separately trained U-Net identifies regions where the vessel appears abnormally narrow — a sign of possible blockage.

4
Lesion Extraction

Detected narrow regions are grouped into distinct lesions using contour detection, each getting its own bounding box.

5
Severity Scoring

For each lesion, we compare the vessel's width at the narrowest point against its normal width nearby — the percentage difference is the stenosis severity.

Both models were trained and evaluated on the ARCADE coronary angiography dataset. This is a screening aid, not a diagnostic device — always confirm findings with a cardiologist.
