How Was This Diagnosed?
1
Image Preprocessing

Your angiogram is resized and normalized to match the format the AI models were trained on.

2
Vessel Segmentation

A U-Net neural network (ResNet34 backbone) traces the coronary vessel tree pixel-by-pixel.

3
Stenosis Segmentation

A second, separately trained U-Net identifies regions where the vessel appears abnormally narrow.

4
Lesion Extraction

Detected narrow regions are grouped into distinct lesions using contour detection.

5
Severity & Confidence Scoring

Each lesion's width is compared against normal vessel width nearby to score severity, alongside a model confidence figure reflecting agreement across test-time augmentation passes.

Both models were trained and evaluated on the ARCADE coronary angiography dataset. This is a screening aid, not a diagnostic device.
