Disclaimers

Please read this carefully before using Arterion for any health-related decision.

Not a Medical Diagnosis

Arterion is an AI-assisted screening and triage tool. It does NOT provide a medical diagnosis and must never replace consultation with a qualified cardiologist or physician.

AI Limitations

Our models are trained on the ARCADE dataset and evaluated with real, honest metrics (Dice, IoU, Precision, Recall) which we publish transparently. No AI model is perfect — false positives and false negatives are possible. Confidence scores reflect the model's own uncertainty and should be read as a signal, not a guarantee.

Always Consult a Professional

Any finding, severity score, or suggestion generated by Arterion should be confirmed by a licensed cardiologist before any treatment decision is made. If you are experiencing chest pain, shortness of breath, or other cardiac symptoms, seek emergency medical care immediately — do not wait for or rely on an AI analysis.

Data & Privacy

Images uploaded for analysis during this demo are processed transiently for inference and are not used for any purpose beyond generating your report. This is a hackathon prototype; production-grade data handling, storage, and consent workflows would be implemented before any real clinical deployment.

Multilingual Reports

Reports in Hindi and Marathi are generated using an AI language model and are provided for accessibility. In case of any discrepancy between languages, the clinical (English/doctor) summary should be treated as the reference version.

Research & Educational Use

This project was built for the Synapse Hackathon (AI in Healthcare track) as a demonstration of applied machine learning in cardiac imaging. It has not undergone clinical trials or regulatory approval (e.g. CDSCO, FDA) and is not licensed as a medical device.
