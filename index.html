The Challenge

During a cath lab procedure, doctors examine live X-ray angiograms of the heart's blood vessels to spot blockages. Reading these images accurately, under time pressure, while a patient is on the table, is mentally demanding and prone to human variability. A system that automatically highlights narrowed or blocked vessel sections would let doctors judge severity faster and with greater confidence.

01

High Cognitive Load

Interpreting fast-moving angiogram footage in real time leaves little room for error under pressure.

02

Time-Critical Decisions

Severity of a blockage must be judged within seconds to guide the next step of the procedure.

03

Subtle Vessel Narrowing

Minor stenosis can be easy to miss on a live feed, especially in low-contrast or noisy frames.

Our Solution

An AI Co-Pilot for the Cath Lab

CoroVision AI processes live angiogram images and video, automatically segmenting vessels and flagging narrowed regions in real time.

Live Video Analysis

Processes streaming angiogram footage frame-by-frame with minimal latency, keeping pace with the live procedure.

Vessel Segmentation

Deep learning models trace the coronary vessel tree, isolating each branch for precise structural analysis.

Blockage Highlighting

Narrowed or occluded segments are automatically outlined and color-coded by severity for instant visual clarity.

Severity Scoring

Quantifies percentage stenosis per segment, giving doctors an objective, consistent severity metric.

Real-Time Overlay

Results are rendered directly on the live X-ray feed, requiring no interruption to the doctor's workflow.

Clinical Reliability

Built to support — not replace — clinical judgment, with confidence indicators on every detection.

How It Works

From Live Feed to Clinical Insight

A streamlined four-stage pipeline turns raw angiogram footage into actionable, real-time guidance.

STEP 1

Ingest Feed

Captures live angiogram image or video stream directly from the cath lab imaging system.

STEP 2

Preprocess & Enhance

Denoises frames and enhances vessel contrast to prepare footage for segmentation.

STEP 3

AI Segmentation

Deep neural network traces vessel structure and detects abnormal narrowing in real time.

STEP 4

Overlay & Alert

Highlights blockages directly on screen with severity scores for immediate clinical review.
